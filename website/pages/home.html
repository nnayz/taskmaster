Questions

Is Taskmaster free?
Yes. Taskmaster is free and open source under Apache-2.0. You pay for the agents you use, through the accounts you already have.
Which agents does it run?
Any terminal agent Taskmaster has a manifest for, including Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok, OpenCode, and Gemini. A missing binary stays in the list with an install hint. Anything without a manifest opens as a shell.
What happens when I quit?
The window closes. The Engine keeps the PTYs. Reopen Taskmaster and the sessions are still there. Agents that support resume can continue a conversation after a clean exit.
Can agents work together?
Yes. A hosted agent can spawn another session, read its output, and answer its questions through Taskmaster MCP. The children show up under the parent in the sidebar.
Does Taskmaster upload my prompts?
There is no Taskmaster account and no telemetry. Agents run on your Mac or on SSH hosts you configure. Logs on that Mac can still contain prompts and secrets a process printed.
Which Macs are supported?
macOS 15 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel, from one universal DMG. Current builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized, and not installed with Homebrew. The in-app updater will not offer them.
